Sentiment Analysis of Gun Ownership without a License

Understanding public sentiments and opinions on the recently implemented legislation regarding gun ownership through NLP techniques.

Overview

In this NLP project, we applied Vader, a Natural Language Processing approach, to perform sentiment analysis on Twitter posts from individuals in Indiana, United States. Our goal was to gain insights into their opinions and emotions regarding the newly implemented legislation on gun ownership without a license.

Examining Public Sentiments

Pre and Post-Implementation Analysis

To comprehensively understand the sentiments surrounding gun ownership, we conducted a two-phase examination. We analyzed sentiments before and after the law's commencement, enabling us to identify any shifts or changes in public sentiment.

Sentiment Trends

Our analysis revealed a significant 70% increase in negative sentiment among individuals following the implementation of the gun ownership law. This finding indicates a notable shift in public perception and highlights potential concerns or dissatisfaction regarding the legislation.

Conclusion

Through our NLP project, we gained valuable insights into the sentiments and opinions of individuals in Indiana regarding the legislation on gun ownership without a license. The observed 70% increase in negative sentiment following the implementation of the law indicates the need for further examination and potential areas for improvement in the legislation.
